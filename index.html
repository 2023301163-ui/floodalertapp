What the warnings mean

Yellow: 7.5 to 15 mm in 1 hourHeavy rain. Flooding is possible. Monitor the weather condition.
Orange: 15 to 30 mm in 1 hourIntense rain. Flooding is threatening. Be alert for possible evacuation.
Red: more than 30 mm in 1 hourSerious flooding expected in low-lying areas. Evacuation.
